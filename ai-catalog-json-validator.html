Structural checks

Every entry needs an identifier, display name, media type, and exactly one artifact delivery mechanism: a remote url or inline data. Duplicate identifiers and invalid timestamps are also reported.

Discovery quality checks

Descriptions should explain when to select the resource. Two to five realistic representative queries give registries stronger text for semantic ranking than a short tag list alone.
